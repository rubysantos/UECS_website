Academic Information

United Evangelical Church School

Learn more about our grading policies, honor roll, report cards, and graduation requirements.

📚 Grading Policies and Standards

  • The student's grade is based on quizzes, classroom outputs, homework, projects, research, behavior, and other school requirements.
  • The cumulative grading system is used. Final term grades consist of 70% of the current term and 30% of the previous term.
  • The passing grade for every subject is 75%, while the lowest possible recorded mark is 50%.
  • A student must achieve at least 60% of the total test items to pass.

📄 Report Cards & Progress Reports

  • Report cards are released twice during the school year.
  • Parents are encouraged to meet with teachers to discuss their child's academic performance.
  • Progress reports are issued every 9th week for students at risk of failing.
  • The lowest passing grade is 75, while the lowest recorded failing grade is 70.

🏆 Honor and Awards

Consistent Honor Roll

  • With Highest Honors: 98 and above
  • With High Honors: 95–97
  • With Honors: 90–94

To qualify:

  • No more than two major violation citations.
  • General average of at least 90% in all three terms.
  • No grade lower than 84% (1st term), 85% (2nd term), and 86% (3rd term) respectively.

Annual Honor Roll

Awarded to students who successfully obtained achiever status during the final term.

🎓 Graduation & Certificate of Completion

  • Students must complete all DepEd and UECS curriculum requirements.
  • Students with incomplete or failed subjects are not allowed to participate in graduation ceremonies until all requirements are completed.
  • Deadlines for projects and school requirements are final and will not be extended.

⭐ Graduation Honors Standards

  • No final grade lower than 86% in any subject.
  • Must demonstrate good moral character.
  • Must have no major disciplinary infractions.
  • Must have shown active leadership from the previous school year until graduation.
