📅 Class Attendance and Absences

  • Regular attendance is required from the first day of regular classes until Graduation or Moving-Up Day.
  • Students whose absences exceed 20% of the prescribed school days may receive a failing grade and lose credit for the subject.
  • Absences for illness must be supported by a medical certificate. Absences due to the death of an immediate family member may also be considered excused.
  • Parents or guardians must submit an Excuse Slip for absences. Students may also need clearance from the Guidance Counselor before returning to class.
  • Birthday celebrations, family trips, and other non-approved reasons are considered invalid excuses.
  • Unexcused absences may result in penalties for activities missed during the absence.
  • Students who leave campus before dismissal must secure permission from the Guidance Counselor.
  • Pre-arranged absences must be requested at least two days before the expected absence.
  • Students are responsible for completing missed assignments and examinations after returning to school.
  • During remedial or summer classes, a maximum of two absences is allowed for a particular subject.

⏰ Lateness / Tardiness

Students are considered late when they are not present during the first bell at 7:20 a.m.

Repeated lateness results in progressively stricter disciplinary actions.

Number of Lates Disciplinary Action
3rd instance Verbal warning and parent contact
4th–6th instance 1 day cleaning duty and parent contact
7th–8th instance 2 days cleaning duty and parent contact
9th–10th instance 1 day out-of-school suspension and parent conference
11th instance 2 days out-of-school suspension and parent conference
12th instance 3 days out-of-school suspension, parent conference, and loss of enrollment privileges

👔 Student Uniform and Appearance

Students are expected to maintain a neat and appropriate appearance that supports a positive learning environment.

  • The prescribed school uniform must be worn properly and with respect.
  • Students must be properly groomed and wear appropriate footwear.
  • An excuse slip must be obtained when a student cannot wear the prescribed uniform because of an emergency.
  • The school ID must always be worn while inside the campus.
  • Clothing that is revealing, offensive, vulgar, or promotes gangs, drugs, alcohol, or tobacco is prohibited.
  • Makeup and hair dye are not allowed except when required for school activities.
  • After PE classes, students may wear the prescribed UECS Family Day shirt or a white shirt.
  • Male students are expected to follow the school's haircut guidelines.

🏫 Campus Hours

Student supervision is available from 7:20 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. on weekdays, except during officially scheduled activities.

Students are expected to leave the campus by 4:30 p.m. Students who need to stay later for school-related activities must have an approved written request.

Disciplinary Actions for Repeated Violations

Offense Action
3rd offense 15–25 minutes cleaning duty
6th offense 15–25 minutes cleaning duty for 2 school days
9th offense 15–25 minutes cleaning duty for 3 school days
12th or more 15–25 minutes cleaning duty for 4 school days

📱 Cellular Phones and Electronic Devices

Students may bring cellular phones to campus, but phones must be surrendered to the classroom adviser during class hours and may be retrieved after school.

Laptops, cameras, audio players, and other electronic devices may only be used in class with the teacher's permission. Devices may be confiscated when school rules are violated.

📚 Materials or Work Left at Home

Students are responsible for bringing their books, assignments, projects, and other school materials. The school will not accept items left at home through the school guard.

Students are encouraged to check their school materials before going to bed to avoid forgetting important requirements.

🌧️ Suspension of Classes

  • Official and legal holidays declared by the Philippine government will be observed.
  • Classes are automatically suspended when Tropical Cyclone Wind Signal No. 2 is raised, following applicable DepEd rules.
  • Official DepEd and LGU announcements regarding suspension of classes will be observed.
  • When there is no official announcement regarding severe weather or transportation concerns, parents may decide whether their child should attend.
  • Parents should inform the school when their child is absent.
